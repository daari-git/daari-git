## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I'm an electrical engineer from **Kathmandu, Nepal** working at the intersection of **power systems and machine learning**.

- ⚡ **Currently:** Associate Electrical Engineer, DER Department at **K&A Power**, running interconnection studies for **Con Edison** and **Orange & Rockland**
- 📄 **Published:** two peer-reviewed papers on ML-based transmission-line fault classification and location (**IET GTD** · **IEEE ICTP**)
- 🎓 **Education:** B.E. Electrical & Electronics, Nepal Engineering College, 2025 ·
- 🧑‍🏫 **Taught:** PLC & SCADA in Industrial Automation as Visiting Faculty at nec
- 🔍 **Interested in:** protection & fault analytics · DER / renewable integration · hosting capacity · EMT & overvoltage analysis
